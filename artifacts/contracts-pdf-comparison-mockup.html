4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 In consideration of the Services, the Client shall pay the fees set out in Schedule A. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes and reimbursable expenses approved in writing by the Client.

4.2 The Provider shall issue invoices monthly in arrears. Each undisputed invoice is due within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

4.3 If the Client disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Provider within ten (10) business days and pay all undisputed amounts when due.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests in accordance with the priorities set out in Schedule B.

5.2 The Provider shall maintain service availability of 99.5% in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance and events outside its reasonable control.

5.3 Service credits are the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for a failure to meet the availability commitment.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier under this section.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.
